
# AI-Based Missing Person Identification and Case Management System

Project Overview

The AI-Based Missing Person Identification and Case Management System is an intelligent platform designed to modernize the way missing-person cases are reported, identified, tracked, and managed. The project combines Artificial Intelligence, facial recognition, image processing, case management, and geographic tracking to create a centralized system for authorities and organizations involved in missing-person investigations. Rather than functioning only as a facial-recognition application, the system provides an end-to-end workflow from case registration to verification and case closure.

Multi-Role Access

The platform introduces multi-role authentication for Government Authorities, Police/Law Enforcement, NGOs, Hospitals/Shelters, Family/Guardians, Public Reporters, and System Administrators. Each role receives appropriate access according to its responsibilities. Citizens can also report possible sightings through a quick public-reporting facility without mandatory account creation, making public participation easier while maintaining structured access for authorized organizations. 

Intelligent Case Registration

Authorized users can register missing-person cases by providing information such as name, age, gender, date missing, last-seen location, city, state, description, police station, and complaint number. The system supports multiple photographs, including front-face, left-profile, right-profile, recent, and older photographs. Before matching, uploaded images undergo quality analysis for face detection, blur, brightness, face angle, and overall suitability. This prevents unsuitable images from directly entering the facial-matching process. 

 AI-Based Matching and Human Verification

The AI matching module compares facial information and generates potential matches with confidence levels. Results can be prioritized based on their matching confidence and image quality. Importantly, the system does not automatically declare someone as found simply because the AI produces a high-confidence result. A potential match proceeds through authorized officer review and verification before the person is confirmed as located and the case is closed or marked as reunited. 

Sighting and Duplicate Case Intelligence

The system includes sighting management, allowing photographs, videos, locations, dates, times, and descriptions of possible sightings to be submitted. The submitted information can pass through image extraction, face detection, AI matching, and police verification. 

Duplicate-case detection further improves case management by identifying situations where the same missing person has been registered independently by police, family members, or NGOs. It compares details such as name, age, gender, location, and facial similarity. 

Key Features

* Multi-role authentication and role-based dashboards
* Multiple-image upload and image-quality checking
* AI-powered facial matching with confidence levels
* Mandatory human verification of potential matches
* Sighting reporting and management
* Duplicate-case detection
* Advanced case-status tracking and filtering
* Geographic mapping and location timelines
* Case analytics and detailed case histories

Project Impact

Overall, the project transforms conventional facial recognition into a structured AI-assisted investigation platform. By connecting missing-person records, potential sightings, intelligent facial matching, geographic information, and authorized human verification, the system aims to make missing-person investigations more organized, efficient, collaborative, and reliable.
